# Round — demo film storylines

The one idea: **in a savings circle, your locked Bitcoin keeps your promise, so the group gets paid even when someone stops paying.**
Spine (all three): one real circle, 4 people × 100 MUSD, 5-minute rounds. Member A takes the $300 pot in round 1, then skips round 2.
Round's BTC covers it: 0.001375 BTC is taken from A's collateral and B still gets paid (200 MUSD + 0.001292 BTC). These are the numbers from our real run.

---

## A. The empty chair — recommended (people first: a stranger follows four friends, not a diagram)

**Device:** a moment that creates the need, with the circle itself as the explanation. **Form:** one frame that rebuilds: the ring
(Round's logo) never leaves the screen. It becomes the table of friends, then the contract, then the logo.

Four people sit around a ring with a pot in the middle. Everyone pays, one person takes the pot, and it feels warm and familiar.
Then A takes the pot and their seat goes dark. That's the moment savings circles break. Round enters, the ring fills with BTC,
and when A misses round 2 an orange coin slides out of A's locked Bitcoin into the pot. B still gets paid.

- **First 2 s:** four seats on a mint ring around "$300"; one by one each seat drops in "100 MUSD" (pop, pop, pop). Line: *Four friends. $100 each. One pot.*
- **Beats:** hook (the circle works) → tension (A takes the pot, seat goes dark, *"This is where circles break"*) → reveal (the ring
  becomes the Round mark, title) → proof in the real app: **lock BTC** (Mezo's price feed sizes it) → **pay with one signature** →
  **settle** → payoff: round 2, BTC flows from A's collateral, B gets the pot (the big moment) → claim *"Your Bitcoin keeps the
  promise"* → end card: round · Built on Mezo · MUSD · contract on testnet.
- **Look:** the site itself: forest-to-mint gradient, Inter Light titles, white cards, navy buttons, the ring. Bitcoin orange is used
  once, on the coin that saves the round.
- **Sound:** near-silence and a low held tone on the empty chair. A warm organic-percussion bed (~110 bpm, a nod to ajo/susu
  communities) enters at the reveal. A riser into an impact as the BTC coin lands. Real clicks on UI presses, pops on payments.

## B. Follow the hundred

**Device:** the worked example: one 100 MUSD payment, followed. **Form:** object-to-object chain: the camera rides one coin the whole film.

We press "Pay 100 MUSD" on a phone and the camera follows that one coin through the signature, into the contract and into the pot.
Then out to the recipient, round after round. When a payment doesn't come, the camera finds the gap and follows a Bitcoin coin out
of the defaulter's vault to fill it.

- **First 2 s:** macro on a finger pressing **Pay 100 MUSD**; the coin pops off the button and the camera chases it.
- **Beats:** press → coin travels (signature, contract) → pot fills → payout → round 2: a missing coin, empty slot → BTC vault opens,
  coin fills the gap → recipient paid → claim → end card.
- **Look:** macro UI and big tactile coins on the mint gradient, depth of field on the app, MUSD green and one BTC orange.
- **Sound:** a tight minimal pulse (~120 bpm); every coin hand-off is a soft tick; the gap gets one beat of silence, then the riser.

## C. Say it big — the bold one

**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** cut on the beat: giant kinetic words and colour fields, with real UI in between.

A fast, confident manifesto: *SAVE. TOGETHER. IN DOLLARS.* — *KEEP YOUR BITCOIN.* Then a question hits: *What if someone stops
paying?* The answer arrives as fragments of the real app cut on the beat: the lock, the signature, the settle, the BTC covering it.

- **First 2 s:** full-bleed forest green, the word **SAVE** slams in, cut to mint, **TOGETHER**, cut to navy, **IN DOLLARS**.
- **Beats:** manifesto → the question → four UI hits (lock · pay · settle · covered) → the default number lands → claim → end card.
- **Look:** full-bleed colour fields from the brand (forest, mint, navy, one orange), Inter at 300/700, hard cuts, no gradients
  except the end card.
- **Sound:** punchy, bright, 124 bpm with a drop at the question; every cut on the beat, no extra effects except the riser into the
  BTC hit.
